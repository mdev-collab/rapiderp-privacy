2. What we collect

Account information

When you sign in with Google, we receive and store your name, email address, profile picture URL, and the fact that you signed in via Google. We do not receive or store your Google password.

Usage information

To understand how the app is used and how many people use it, we record:

  • A session record each time you open the app
  • A one-time install record for your device
  • Your platform (Android or iOS) and the app version
  • The date and time you last used the app

Push notification token

If you allow notifications, we store an anonymous push token for your device so we can send you alerts. You can revoke this at any time in your device settings.

Business data — stored only on your device

Your business data never leaves your phone. Products, sales, orders, invoices, customers, employees, salaries, attendance and settings are saved in the app’s private storage on your device only. We cannot see this data, and it is not uploaded to our servers or to anyone else.

Because this data is stored locally, it is deleted when you uninstall the app, and it is not backed up or transferred by us. Keep your own records of anything you cannot afford to lose.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • We do not collect payment card numbers or bank details.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

The app lets you record details about your employees (such as names, roles and salaries) and your customers (such as names and phone numbers). This information stays on your device and is never sent to us.

You are responsible for that information. If you use Rapid ERP to store details about staff or customers, you must have a lawful basis to do so, tell them how you use their information, and honour their rights under the privacy laws that apply to your business.

6. Where your data is stored

Account and usage data is stored on Supabase infrastructure and may be processed in countries other than your own. Business data stays on your device.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account information — kept while your account exists. Deleted when you ask us to delete your account.
  • Session and install records — kept for up to 24 months, then deleted.
  • Push tokens — kept until you turn off notifications or delete your account.
  • Business data — controlled entirely by you; removed when you uninstall the app.

8. Security

Access to your account data is protected by authentication and database access rules that restrict each account to its own records. Data travels over encrypted HTTPS connections. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.
